\section{Provenance narrative}
The theorem line is assembled from a fixed project spine.
The theorem order and section homes come from \texttt{docs/project/theorem\_atlas.yaml}.
The theorem statements correspond to \texttt{docs/project/theorem\_statement\_sheet.yaml}, whose Lean scopes are recorded in \texttt{review/current-theorem-coverage.json}.
The notation registry \texttt{docs/project/notation\_registry.yaml} records the version~1 conventions; where it differs from the main paper (for example in allowing only positive lags or in its handling of support conditions for KL divergence), the main paper is authoritative.

Claim-level bindings are frozen in \texttt{docs/project/claim\_audit\_freeze.yaml} and \texttt{docs/project/theorem\_evidence\_map.yaml}.
Asset choices are frozen in \texttt{docs/project/paper\_asset\_freeze.yaml} and \texttt{docs/project/caption\_registry.yaml}.
Readiness and formal-support status were tracked in \texttt{docs/project/readiness\_checklist.yaml} together with the per-front Lean bridge files under \texttt{docs/project/lean\_*.yaml}; these are historical records, and the current formal-support status is the coverage receipt described in the formalization support map below.

The supplement tables do not introduce new mathematical claims.
The claim, asset, and callout tables in the theorem--evidence map are version~1 records: they document the frozen claim bindings and planned assets of that version, and their figure and table identifiers and status descriptions do not refer to the figures or formal-support status of the version~2 main paper.
