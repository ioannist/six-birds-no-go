\section{Formalization support map}
All eight theorem fronts are formalized directly in Lean~4 (toolchain \texttt{v4.28.0}, Mathlib pinned to the matching release).
The current coverage receipt is \texttt{review/current-theorem-coverage.json}; it records the exported declarations, their precise scope, source hashes, and the results of a clean root build, explicit target-type checks (\texttt{review/CheckTargets.lean}), and a transitive axiom audit (\texttt{review/CheckAxioms.lean}).
The declarations below depend only on \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
The receipt is regenerated by \texttt{python3 scripts/run\_mathematical\_audit.py}, and the semantic review of each formal statement is in \texttt{review/mathematical-review.txt}.
